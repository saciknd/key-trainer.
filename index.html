How it works

Building an answer

In chord mode, tap a root letter (C, D, E, F, G, A, B) to expand it, then tap a chord to add it to the tray. In scale mode, tap the notes in scale order. You can remove a chord by tapping the × on its chip, or use Undo and Clear.

Answers sync across devices

Every chord you add or remove is saved to a Google Sheet. If you have the app open on another device, it will update within about 5 seconds — no refresh needed. So you can start an answer on your phone and finish it on your laptop.

Check = submit

Pressing Check commits whatever is in the tray at that moment. It's like handing in an exam paper.

  • All seven correct → ✓ correct, and the worked solution opens.
  • Any wrong or missing → ✗ wrong. The chips show your answer colour-coded: green for right, red for wrong (with the correct chord underneath), and grey dashed for missing (with the correct chord underneath). The worked solution opens too.

You can't edit after Check — but you can read the solution for as long as you like, then press New question.

New question = submit and advance

If you press New question without having pressed Check, the current tray is submitted first (scored as wrong if incomplete or wrong, exactly like Check), and then the next question is drawn. The solution does not open in this case — you're moving on, not reviewing.

Skip

Pressing Skip gives up on the current question. It's counted as wrong, and the solution opens.

Reading the worked solution

The solution panel explains how to work out the answer step by step — the key signature, which order of sharps or flats applies, the scale, and the chord grid. For minor keys it also shows the Alphabet Rule for finding the relative major, and for theoretical keys (like C# major or Cb major) it shows the practical enharmonic alternative.

Unfinished questions

Once you press Check or New question, that question is behind you for this round — you can't come back and edit it. The round re-shuffles after all keys have been drawn once.

Reset

Reset (all devices) clears the shared score and round on every device. Your answer history in the Google Sheet is kept.
